How the overall ranking works
  • Each game scores 0–100 by where you finished among that day's players: 1st is always 100, and with 5 players it goes 100, 80, 60, 40, 20. Equal scores share the place. A game counts once two people have posted it.
  • Your daily score is the average of your best 4 games. You need 4 counted games to be ranked that day, so playing more never hurts.
  • Week: average of your best 5 daily scores, with at least 3 ranked days (fewer early in the week). Month: best 20, at least 8. All time: every ranked day, at least 5.
  • Crowns 👑 are 1st places in a single game; 🥇 counts days you finished 1st overall.
